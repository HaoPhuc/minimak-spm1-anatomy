## The six contested questions

- **A. Labels on the model.** Tracking callouts with leader lines (Kai, Janssen), static numbered balloons with no
  lines (Daniel), a single leader only for the hovered or kept sub-part (Sofia, Amara), or none (the old rule)?
- **B. Taking apart.** All sub-parts at once (today), or a stepped, ordered "open this joint / put it on" sequence with
  star-numbered bolts (Ruud, Mai)? Is a stepped player a new mode that Daniel's rules forbid, or the point of the app?
- **C. Enhance.** What gesture or control frames one small sub-part (double-click, `Z`, a "Show me" button on the
  sheet, tap a sheet), and does it need a locator inset (Kai)? It must work by mouse, keyboard, touch and on a beamer.
- **D. Where it goes.** Solid "mate ghost" and framing that keeps the seat in view (Sofia, Daniel), a seat ring and
  drop line (Kai), a tether, a compass / quarter names on the model (Janssen, Mai)? Which one, or which combination?
- **E. Single bolts.** Picking one instance of 20 (Kai, Sofia) makes targets smaller; tap tolerance and a loupe
  (Lena) make them bigger. How do these fit together? Is one bolt ever the right unit, or is the ring?
- **F. Fix the content first?** Ruud's missing gaskets, bolts ending in air, flat knife edges, KF joints. Should the
  hardware be made true before any new UI, alongside it, or after?
