## 3. A–F

- **A. Labels on the model.** Balloons, plus one leader for the kept or hovered sub-part. The owner's new priority overturns the old rule: hover lighting is invisible in Colour.
- **B. Taking apart.** Ordered steps *inside* Take apart, with ‹ › to step and "all at once" as step 0. SolidWorks explode views are ordered steps, so this is convention, not a new mode.
- **C. Enhance.** One function (`frameBox` on the sub-part) reached four ways: double-click, `Z`, **Show me** on the sheet bar (clicker and beamer), and a tap on that bar (touch). Esc restores the previous frame. The inset can wait.
- **D. Where it goes.** A solid mate ghost for the parts in `connects`, framing that keeps the seat in view, and one seat ring with a drop line. Quarter names on the table. No tethers on every sub-part.
- **E. Single bolts.** The ring is the unit, since 20 identical bolts share one sheet. Single bolts matter only for star order, so number them inside Open this joint. I demote my own instance picking.
- **F. Fix the content first?** Alongside the UI, and first within each phase. The mate ghost and the section will *expose* the missing gaskets, so fix them before shipping the view that shows them.
