# Lena: round 2

## 1. I oppose
1. **Picking one bolt by default (Kai's 3, Sofia's 3).** A nut is about 15 px on my tablet and 6–7 px on a phone, where only 15 % of touch points near one hit it. Picking by instance makes every target 20× smaller, for a sheet that is the same on all 20. *Instead:* pick the ring, and reach single bolts after Enhance (heads at least 30 px) or through balloons.
2. **Double-click as *the* way to enhance (Daniel's 3; also Kai, Sofia, Ruud).** With gloves, a double-tap on a 7 px nut is two misses, and double-tap already means inspect. *Instead:* a 44 px **Show me** button on every sheet (Amara's idea), with double-click and `Z` as shortcuts.
3. **The camera turning by itself on take-apart (Kai's 5, "orbit until ≥60°").** Inspect keeps my azimuth, and that is how I match the screen to the machine from where I stand. *Instead:* keep my turn, and offer a **Turn to see** button only when the take-apart axis is within about 20° of my line of sight.

## 2. I support
1. **Amara's 4, tremor tolerance:** 10 px slop, the smallest sub-part within 12 px, triad targets of 24 px. It is my P1 from another angle; merge it with my first proposal.
2. **Sofia's 1, the mate ghost:** joined parts drawn solid, with the seat kept in frame. "Where does the one in my hand go?" is goal 2. It **outranks** my proposals 3 and 5.
3. **Janssen's 1, deep links,** printed as QR stickers on the real SPM1: scan one and the part opens with its sheet, with no picking. It **outranks** my proposal 4 for "what is this in front of me?".

## 3. A–F
- **A. Labels.** Daniel's numbered balloons, plus one leader line from the kept or previewed sheet (Sofia, Amara). The old rejection rests on "hover lighting is enough", and a tablet has no hover. Make every balloon at least 44 px: **the label becomes the target, not the nut.**
- **B. Taking apart.** Steps, yes: one step per screen with a big **Next**, as ‹ › inside Take apart, not as a new mode.
- **C. Enhance.** **Show me**, double-click and `Z`. The inset appears only beyond about 4× zoom; tap it to go back.
- **D. Where it goes.** A mate ghost with a seat ring (Sofia, Kai), and Front, Right, Back and Left on the table (Janssen), so I can match my side of the bench. No camera moves by itself.
- **E. Single bolts.** The ring is the unit for picking and for sheets. One bolt matters only for the tightening order: star-numbered balloons, after Enhance.
- **F. Content.** Fix it alongside the UI, starting with the missing DN40 gaskets and the KF joints. The O-ring I hold must be on screen.

## 4. My revised top 3
1. **Finger-tolerant picking, with balloons as the targets** (mine, Amara's 4, Daniel's 2).
2. **Show me plus one leader line,** with the part framed below the inspect bar (Amara's 2, Sofia's leader line, my framing fix).
3. **Mate context:** Sofia's ghost, Kai's seat ring, Janssen's quarter names, and the camera keeps my turn.
