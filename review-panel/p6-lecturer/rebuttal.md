## 1. I oppose
1. **Daniel's unnamed numbered balloons.** In a hall, a "3" sends 120 people to a 12.5 px list they can't read. *Instead:* named balloons ("3 · KF16 clamp") sitting on the part, leader only where needed, at presenter scale.
2. **Kai's auto-orbit on Take apart, and the locator inset.** I turn the model to match the real SPM1 in front of the class. 3. **Sofia's trail line per bolt.** A joint has 20 bolts and 20 nuts: 40 hairlines that project as grey hatching. *Instead:* one trail per sub-part set, at least 2 px at 1080.

## 3. My position on A–F
- **A. Named labels on the model,** laid out when the camera stops (Amara is right about drift), plus one leader from the kept sheet. The "no arrows" rule assumed one person with a mouse. A hall has no hover; the owner's Stark priority overturns the rule.
- **B. Stepped: yes,** as ‹ › inside Take apart rather than a new mode, with star numbers on the ring. Fitting order is what students need before they bolt the real joint, and each step is a key press I can narrate.
- **C. Enhance:** double-click, `Z` and a **Show me** button on the sheet should all give the same frame, about 50% of the view. Esc glides back; no inset needed. At the lectern I'd use **Show me**.
- **D. Where it goes:** Sofia's solid mate, Kai's seat ring, quarter names and a front mark, with the seat kept in frame. Leave out the tether: one cue per job.
- **E. The ring is the unit.** All 20 bolts are identical, so one bolt matters only as a star number in B. Lena's tolerance first: it makes the ring easier to hit.
- **F. Alongside, but the missing gaskets and air-ended bolts go first** (S, and wrong today). Knife edges and KF joints ship with Enhance, because ×16 exposes flat faces.
