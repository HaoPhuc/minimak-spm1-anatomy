# Round 2: Amara, accessibility

## 1. I oppose

1. **Kai's #1: callouts on every sub-part, redrawn during an orbit.** At 200% the model gets about 420×360 px (`z2`), and ten labels with lines would bury it. A magnifier user cannot follow text that slides, and there is no keyboard path between labels. *Accept:* one line for the hovered, **focused** or kept sub-part, drawn only at rest. Daniel's static balloons, matching the *Made of* numbers, could be an optional layer.
2. **Kai's #3 and Sofia's #3: one bolt instance as the unit you select.** One instance is a 22–34 px target. In my tremor run, a click 16 px off already kept the wrong sheet. It also adds a fifth level that keyboard and screen-reader users must step through, for a sheet that is the same on all 20. *Accept:* "bolt 7 of 20" shown on hover, and one bolt lit by a step (B), not by aim.
3. **Kai's #5: an automatic orbit to put the axis ≥60° from the line of sight on take-apart.** A camera that moves unasked is a vestibular risk, and a magnifier user loses their place. Daniel bans this too. *Accept:* a **Turn to show** button that cuts instantly under reduced motion.

## 2. I support

1. **Lena: finger-tolerant picking** (a 24 px ray disc that prefers the smallest sub-part; a miss keeps the preview) **and framing below the inspect bar** (`z2` puts the gauge under the bar). This is my tremor fix, better specified, and it **replaces my #4**.
2. **Daniel: one meaning per click** (a click keeps a sheet, only × or the row closes it). A tremor's second click no longer undoes work. It **merges into my #5**.
3. **Janssen: presenter `--ui-scale` and deep links.** Low-vision users get larger text without the stacked layout, and anyone can resume where they were. It **outranks my #5**.

## 3. A–F

- **A.** One leader line, for the hovered, focused or kept sub-part, hidden during an orbit. A line drawn at rest is not "arrows that move as you turn", so the owner's new priority overturns the old rejection.
- **B.** Steps inside Take apart, not a new mode. ‹ › and `,` `.` step. Each step is announced with its sentence, and star numbers are text, not colour.
- **C.** One command, three bindings: **Show me** on each sheet and *Made of* row (keyboard, touch, beamer), `Z`, and double-click. The inset is static and never takes focus. Esc goes back. It cuts under reduced motion.
- **D.** Sofia and Daniel's solid mates, framed in view. A dashed seat ring of at least 3:1. Quarter names as text on the table and in the view's description. No animated tether.
- **E.** The ring is the unit. Make targets bigger (Lena). The instance number is information, not something to aim at.
- **F.** Alongside. Ruud's gaskets are true content, but an invisible highlight hides whatever is drawn.

## 4. Revised top 3

1. **Visible lit states in Colour** (mine). Mai, Ruud, Daniel and Lena found it too.
2. **Show me / enhance with one leader line, fired by focus as well as hover.** This merges my #2 with Kai's #2, Sofia's #3, Daniel's #3, Lena's #4 and her framing below the bar.
3. **Tolerant picking (Lena) with one meaning per click (Daniel).**

Each ships with its line in the live region (my #3).
