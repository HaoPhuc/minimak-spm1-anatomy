# Ruud (vacuum technician): round 2

## I oppose

1. **Single-bolt picking ("Bolt 7 of 20"; Kai, and Sofia's #3).** Nobody opens a CF joint one bolt at a time: the ring comes off together, in star order. Single picks shrink 15 px targets that Lena hits only 38 % of the time, and they cost an M while gaskets are missing. *I'd accept:* the ring stays the thing you pick, numbered 1–20 in star order within the joint sequence, with one sample bolt dimensioned (length, AF, thread; tagged).
2. **Balloons without leaders (Daniel).** The stack has six bolt rings within 3 cm. A floating "4" names none of them after a 90° turn, and drawing balloons always have leaders. *I'd accept:* his balloons, at most 10, numbered like the **Made of** rows, each with a short leader.
3. **Lifting the gasket when inspected (Kai).** A CF gasket can't leave a closed joint: nuts, bolts and one flange come off first. Lifting it teaches a move that nicks knife edges. *I'd accept:* the gasket stays seated, and its Take apart opens the whole joint, gasket last.

## I support

1. **Mai's "Put it on" step player inside Take apart.** It is my "open this joint", cheaper, and it lives in an existing button, so it is not the new mode Daniel fears. It should cover the whole joint: flanges, gasket, bolts and nuts. **It outranks my #2.**
2. **Sofia's mate context.** Draw the parts it connects to solid, with their fasteners, and keep the seat in frame. Bolts without their holes are a picture of bolts, not a joint. **It outranks my #4.**
3. **Amara's lit states in Colour (kept = fill, preview = dashed).** My `r3-h3` shows the preview is invisible on grey bolts. **It outranks my #5.**

## My position on A–F

- **A. Labels:** one leader on the hovered or kept sub-part, plus leadered balloons while a piece is apart. Hover lighting failed on steel for Mai, Amara and me. The Stark priority overturns "no arrows".
- **B. Take apart:** stepped and in order, inside Take apart. That is the point of the app, not a new mode.
- **C. Enhance:** one function behind three triggers: a double-click, `Z`, or **Show me** on the sheet bar for touch and the beamer. Keep the seat solid. The inset can come later; the scale bar matters more.
- **D. Where it goes:** solid mates and a framed seat (Sofia, Daniel), then Kai's seat ring, plus quarter names on the table so "right–back" means something.
- **E. Single bolts:** you pick the ring. A single bolt only gets dimensions and a sequence number. Lena's finger tolerance and loupe come first.
- **F. Content:** fix it first. The gaskets and nuts take a day, and a step player would put the wrong order on screen. Knife edges come with Sofia's section.
