# Mai: round 3

## 1. Verdict

**Accept with changes.** Phase 0 first, V1 at ≥3:1, the ring as the unit, and the stepper inside Take apart are all what I argued for.

## 2. Blocking objections

1. **The stepper must say how, not only show.** O1's "one sentence per step" could end up as a mere caption.
   - *Change:* each step names the part, which way it faces, and the thread, bolt length and spanner (from T4). The gasket step says "new gasket, never reused".
   - *O1 done-when:* "A first-year recites the viewport fitting order (bolt size, spanner, star order) from the app alone in under 2 min."
2. **Nothing is checked on a student laptop.** At 1366×768 with the column open, the model gets about 630 px (m03). S1, S3 and W1 could pass at 1440 and fail there.
   - *Change:* give S1, S3 and W1 one done-when at 1366×768, trackpad, column open:
     - the sensor nut is framed at ≥50 % of the free view;
     - at most 10 callouts, none overlapping or covering the part;
     - the KF16 seat stays in frame after Take apart.

## 3. Move earlier

**Move O1 and O2 to just after Phase 1.** They need only T1–T4 and V1, and fitting order is what I need at the bench next week. "Show me" on a bolt doesn't tell me when to fit it. Put S4 (single bolts) after O2, since a bolt only means something with its star number.
